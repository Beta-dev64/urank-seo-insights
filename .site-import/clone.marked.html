Frequently asked Questions

  • How does Rank work?

    Rank gives you simple ways to save, access funds, and grow your money, through Savings, Circles, Tribe, and Investments, all in one place.

  • What is Circles?

    A modern, safer version of Ajo. Join a circle, and receive a lump sum when your slot comes. Rank guarantees your payout even if someone else in. The circle defaults. No debt, no loan apps, no stress. It is public Ajo open to everyone.

  • What is Tribe?

    Tribe lets you save and grow money with people you trust. It’s a shared system that helps you stay consistent and reach your goals faster.

  • How does Rank Savings work?

    You choose a savings plan, set your amount, and save at your own pace while your money grows over time.

  • Do I need experience to start investing?

    No. With Rank Wealth Managers, you get expert guidance to help you invest based on your goals and risk level.

  • Is my money safe with Rank?

    Yes. Your funds are protected with bank-grade security, held with licensed custodians, and never mixed with Rank's own money.
